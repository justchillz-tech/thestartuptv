CAMPAIGN TERMS

Internal Startup TV
Referral Campaign.

These terms govern participation in the Startup TV Internal Referral Campaign for LUMOR Film Festival.

01

Campaign Objective

The Startup TV Internal Referral Campaign is intended to use the Startup TV internal community to generate additional film submissions for LUMOR Film Festival by enabling Startup Park team members to refer filmmakers from their personal networks.

02

Campaign Period

The campaign will run from 16 September 2026 through 23 September 2026, inclusive.

Only qualifying referral activity recorded during the campaign period will be considered for the campaign.

03

Eligibility

  • The campaign is open to current Startup Park team members who register through the designated internal referral flow.
  • Each eligible team member may register for their own personal referral code, referral link and QR code.
  • Referral codes and referral links are personal to the registered referrer and may not be transferred to another person.
04

How It Works

  • A Startup Park team member registers through the internal referral flow.
  • The system generates a personal referral code, referral link and associated QR code.
  • The referrer shares the link, QR code or referral code with friends, filmmakers or potential participants.
  • When the referred person opens the referral link, the LUMOR Film Festival submission landing page opens with the referrer's attribution embedded.
  • The filmmaker completes the normal submission process.
  • A valid submitted film is attributed to the referring Startup Park team member.
  • The same referrer may share their referral link, QR code or referral code with multiple people.
05

Referral Attribution

  • Each referred filmmaker will be counted as one unique referred person, regardless of how many films they submit.
  • A filmmaker may be attributed to only one referrer.
  • Where multiple referral claims exist for the same filmmaker, the earliest verifiable referral attribution recorded by the system will be considered the valid attribution.
  • Only referral activity that can be verified through the LUMOR referral system will be considered.
06

Referral Counting Rules

  • Each valid film submission counts as one submission toward the referrer's total.
  • If one referred filmmaker submits multiple valid films, all valid films count toward the referrer's submission total, but that filmmaker remains one unique referred person.
  • A minimum of 3 valid referred film submissions is required to qualify for the Referral Champion Prize.
  • Duplicate, incomplete, cancelled or invalid submissions do not count.
  • Only submissions verifiably attributed through the referral system count toward the campaign.
  • Self-referrals are not permitted and will not count toward the referrer's total.
07

Valid Film Submission

For the purposes of this campaign, a valid film submission is a submission that:

  • is successfully received through the LUMOR Film Festival submission system;
  • is attributable to a registered referral code or link;
  • satisfies the applicable LUMOR Film Festival submission requirements; and
  • is not subsequently identified as duplicate, incomplete, cancelled, fraudulent or otherwise invalid.
08

Referral Winner & Tie-Breakers

  • The Startup Park team member with the highest number of valid referred film submissions wins the ₹3,000 Referral Champion Prize.
  • If two or more referrers have the same total number of valid submissions, the person with the higher number of unique referred filmmakers wins.
EXAMPLE

A refers Z and C. Z submits two films and C submits one film, giving A three submissions from two unique filmmakers.

B refers V, N and M. Each submits one film, giving B three submissions from three unique filmmakers.

B wins because B referred more unique filmmakers.

  • If both the total number of valid submissions and the number of unique referred filmmakers are equal, the person whose qualifying referral/submission activity was recorded first in the system wins.
09

Invalid or Manipulated Activity

  • Duplicate, fraudulent, artificially generated or otherwise manipulated submissions may be excluded from the campaign.
  • Attempts to manipulate referral totals, including submitting duplicate films, creating false identities or otherwise abusing the referral system, may result in disqualification.
  • The organizers reserve the right to investigate and exclude submissions that do not meet the campaign requirements.
10

Winner Verification

  • Referral totals will be determined using the records maintained by the LUMOR referral and submission system.
  • The organizers may verify the underlying submissions and referral attribution before confirming the winner.
  • System records will be used as the basis for determining qualifying submissions, unique referred filmmakers and campaign results.
11

Referral Champion Prize

  • One eligible winner will receive the ₹3,000 Referral Champion Prize.
  • The prize will be provided through the payment method designated by the organizers.
  • Any applicable statutory deductions or requirements will apply.
12

Final Decision

The organizers reserve the right to determine the validity of submissions, referral attribution, eligibility and campaign results based on the available system records and campaign rules.

In the event of any dispute relating to the campaign, the organizers' decision will be final.
