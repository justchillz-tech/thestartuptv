Terms & Conditions

Campaign Period: 16 September 2026 to 23 September 2026, inclusive.

The campaign is open to current Startup Park team members who register through the designated referral flow.

Each registered referrer receives a personal referral code, link and QR code which may be shared with multiple filmmakers or potential participants.

Each valid film submission counts as one submission toward the referrer's total. Multiple valid film submissions by the same filmmaker will all count toward the submission total, but the filmmaker will count as one unique referred person.

A minimum of 3 valid referred film submissions is required to qualify for the Referral Champion Prize.

Duplicate, incomplete, cancelled, fraudulent or otherwise invalid submissions will not count. Self-referrals are not permitted.

A filmmaker may be attributed to only one referrer. Where multiple referral claims exist, the earliest verifiable referral attribution recorded by the system will be considered valid.

The Startup Park team member with the highest number of valid referred film submissions wins the ₹3,000 Referral Champion Prize.

In the event of a tie, the referrer with more unique referred filmmakers wins. If both totals are equal, the qualifying referral/submission activity recorded first in the system will determine the winner.

The organizers reserve the right to verify submissions, referral attribution and eligibility and to exclude activity that violates these terms or attempts to manipulate the referral system.

The organizers' decision regarding eligibility, submission validity, referral attribution and campaign results will be final.
